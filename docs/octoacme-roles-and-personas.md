# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Delivery Leads

### Role Summary
Delivery Leads own the day-to-day execution cadence for a project or workstream. They turn the project plan into coordinated action, monitor progress toward milestones, and surface dependencies or delivery risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Coordinate execution across functional teams and workstreams
- Track milestones, dependencies, blockers, and delivery commitments
- Maintain an actionable delivery board and escalate material risks
- Facilitate execution-focused ceremonies and follow up on decisions
- Provide progress information to the Project Manager for status reporting

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager on schedule health, dependencies, risks, and escalations; the Project Manager retains overall project coordination and stakeholder reporting accountability.
- Work with Product Managers to sequence work against product priorities and clarify delivery trade-offs.
- Coordinate with Developers and Technical Leads to remove blockers without taking ownership of technical decisions.

---

## Technical Leads

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide technical direction and implementation quality for a project or workstream. They help the team make sound design decisions, assess technical risk, and maintain engineering standards.

### Responsibilities
- Guide architecture, design, and implementation decisions
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies and recommend mitigations
- Review technical approaches and support consistent quality standards
- Help Developers resolve complex problems and estimate technical work
- Communicate technical trade-offs, readiness, and quality concerns clearly

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partner with the Project Manager and Delivery Lead to communicate technical risk, feasibility, dependencies, and schedule implications.
- Work with Product Managers to translate product outcomes into feasible technical approaches and explain trade-offs.
- Support Developers through design reviews and technical guidance while preserving their implementation ownership.
- Consult the Business Owner when technical options affect scope, cost, risk, or expected outcomes.

---

## Business Owners

### Role Summary
Business Owners represent the business outcomes and stakeholder priorities that a project is intended to achieve. They provide direction on value, approve significant scope or priority decisions, and help confirm that delivered outcomes meet organizational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Define or validate desired business outcomes and success measures
- Represent business stakeholder priorities and resolve competing needs
- Approve significant scope, priority, or outcome trade-offs within their authority
- Participate in milestone reviews and confirm business readiness
- Provide timely decisions and escalate unresolved organizational constraints

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on customer value, product priorities, and success measures.
- Work with the Project Manager on scope decisions, governance, risks, and stakeholder alignment; the Project Manager remains accountable for coordinating delivery.
- Consult the Technical Lead on feasibility, quality, and technical risk before approving trade-offs.
- Provide the Delivery Lead with outcome priorities and timely decisions needed to keep execution moving.

---

## Program Coordinators

### Role Summary
Program Coordinators support the operating rhythm of projects or related workstreams by organizing information, communication, meetings, and handoffs. They improve visibility and consistency without duplicating decision-making authority held by project, product, or business leaders.

### Responsibilities
- Organize recurring meetings, agendas, notes, action items, and decision records
- Maintain shared documentation, reporting inputs, and communication schedules
- Track cross-workstream handoffs and follow up on agreed actions
- Support risk, dependency, and milestone visibility across related projects
- Help prepare status materials and route information to the appropriate decision maker

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Support the Project Manager with documentation, meeting logistics, status inputs, and follow-up while the Project Manager owns project-level decisions and accountability.
- Coordinate with Delivery Leads to keep milestone, dependency, and blocker information current.
- Route product, business, and technical questions to the Product Manager, Business Owner, or Technical Lead rather than making decisions on their behalf.
- Improve communication for Developers and stakeholders by maintaining a reliable source of project information.

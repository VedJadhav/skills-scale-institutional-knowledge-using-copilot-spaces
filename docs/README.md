# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

A guide to the project management processes, roles, and delivery practices used by OctoAcme.

## Project management process summary

OctoAcme uses a lifecycle-based, iterative approach to project delivery. Initiation validates the problem, desired outcomes, stakeholders, risks, resources, and go/no-go decision. Planning turns approved initiatives into prioritized and estimated backlog items with acceptance criteria, owners, dependencies, milestones, a release plan, and a Definition of Done.

During execution, teams track work on a project board, deliver small pull requests, and use standups, planning sessions, weekly delivery syncs, and sprint or milestone demos to maintain alignment. Project Managers coordinate schedules, risks, dependencies, and communications; Product Managers define outcomes and priorities; Developers build and test solutions; QA validates quality and acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals.

Risk management and communication are continuous practices. Teams maintain a risk register, review risks and dependencies during weekly syncs, share regular status updates, and escalate issues from the team to the Project Manager, Product Lead, and sponsor as impact increases. Releases require completed acceptance criteria, passing CI and security scans, staged smoke testing, post-deployment verification, stakeholder communication, and a rollback or mitigation plan. After sprints, releases, milestones, or incidents, retrospectives capture what went well, what should improve, and a small set of owned, measurable action items.
